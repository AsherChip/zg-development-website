ABOUT ZG DEVELOPMENT

OUR MISSION

Creating Homes with
Purpose.

ZG Real Estate Development LLC aims to develop attractive, energy-conscious, attainable single-family homes in Huntington, Indiana while returning distressed and underutilized land to productive use and strengthening the neighborhoods in which it operates.

OUR APPROACH

Thoughtful Development.
Lasting Value.

Our approach focuses on practical design, quality construction, energy-conscious decisions, and creating homes that people are proud to call their own. We believe thoughtful development can bring new life to underutilized land and strengthen the communities we serve.

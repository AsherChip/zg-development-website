CO-FOUNDER & 50% OWNER

Zachary J. Denman

Zachary J. Denman is a co-founder and 50% owner of ZG Real Estate Development. His background includes U.S. Army service and the completion of Military Police training in July 2026. During Basic Training, he was recognized with a Distinguished Leadership Award.

Zach is currently pursuing a degree in Business Economics at Purdue University Fort Wayne's Doermer School of Business, with a minor in Political Science. He is expected to graduate in 2028 and has also completed coursework in management.

In addition to his military background, Zach has experience as a legal assistant, providing exposure to legal processes, documentation, and professional networks. His leadership and professional experience support ZG's emphasis on accountability, organization, communication, and disciplined execution.

CO-FOUNDER & 50% OWNER

Gunnar J. Inderlied

Gunnar J. Inderlied is a co-founder and 50% owner- operator of ZG Real Estate Development. He began working in excavation at a young age through projects with his father, who has extensive experience in the field. These early experiences helped him develop a strong work ethic and an understanding of the construction process.

Gunnar currently works at First Federal Savings Bank in Huntington as a Commercial Loan Processor and Treasury Management Assistant. Through his work in banking, he has developed an understanding of lending processes, financial controls, cash management, and financial analysis.

Gunnar is pursuing a degree in Finance at Purdue University Fort Wayne's Doermer School of Business. His experience in construction and banking gives him a unique perspective on both the practical and financial sides of real estate development.

Gunnar first entered the real estate industry through wholesaling. As he learned more about the market, he recognized an opportunity to make a more meaningful and lasting contribution to his local community through real estate development. That vision ultimately led to the creation of ZG Real Estate Development and its mission of strengthening communities through property investment.
